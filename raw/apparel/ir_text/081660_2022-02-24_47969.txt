BRAND

STATEMENT

From its humble beginnings in Biella, Italy in 1911 to its historic introduction of color on the tennis court in the 1970s,

FILA has always remained committed to making a statement about individuality, power, and style through product that 16
both novel in aesthetic and effective in function. While the brand’s offerings were originally intended for those defiant of
expectations in sport, it was only natural that limit challengers and subculture creators everywhere would soon recognize
FILA as an aspirational beacon.

It was the introduction of the Original Tennis shoe in 1985 that catapulted FILA from its reputation as an authentic athletic
brand into a genuine lifestyle icon. As the era’s most legendary hip-hop artists and fashion players embraced the brand and
the street-savvy collections it inspired, it became clear to all that FILA was synonymous with the courage to create a lasting
mark in whatever world you compete. Athlete or not, this was a brand as bold and breakthrough as its wearers - a brand to

truly make your own.
After half a century of paving the way in “sport-style”, FILA continues to inspire new generations with modern looks crafted
from its DNA, a game changing heritage both on and off the court.
